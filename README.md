# 🌐 Berkeley Digital Transformation: Leading People, Data & Technology
### Leading People, Data, and Technology

This repository contains my projects and notes from the **Berkeley Executive Education** Digital Transformation program. This course focuses on managing the organizational disruption caused by emerging technologies and data-driven business models.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- **Digital Business Models:** Analyzing subscription-based and platform models for growth.
- **People & Alignment:** Managing the human side of technological change and organizational resistance.
- **Customer-Centric Processes:** Re-engineering process flows through a customer-first digital lens.
- **Ethical Leadership:** Navigating the legal and ethical complexities of digital business and algorithmic bias.

## 🔍 Core Focus Areas
1. **Data:** Using predictive and prescriptive modeling for decision-making.
2. **Technology:** Identifying optimal digital solutions for specific business problems.
3. **People:** Specifying strategic imperatives and KPIs for transformation success.
